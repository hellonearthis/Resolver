# MiniMax H3 Production Brief: Doctor Who Laser Eyes vs Dalek (30s Skit)

## 1. Reference Job Assignments
- `<Picture 1>`: Reference for The Doctor (tall, eccentric figure in a rugged tweed coat, messy hair, expressive wide eyes).
- `<Picture 2>`: Reference for Classic Dalek (bronze metallic shell, eyestalk, plunger and laser appendage).

## 2. Scene / Format / Mood
- **Setting**: A dimly lit, steam-filled metallic corridor in an abandoned Dalek spacecraft, gritty sci-fi cinematic look.
- **Genre & Tone**: British Sci-Fi Comedy, high-energy cinematic realism with comic absurdity.
- **Format**: 4K UHD, 24fps, cinematic 2.39:1 widescreen, natural motion blur.

## 3. One Dominant Action
The Doctor confronts cornering Daleks, squinting hard as dual scorching optic laser beams erupt from his pupils, instantly slagging and liquefying the front Dalek into a molten heap while the remaining Dalek scrambles in reverse.

## 4. Camera Path & Framing
The shot opens at eye level in a medium shot showing the Doctor backed up against a steel bulkhead. As the Daleks advance into the foreground, the camera slowly glides forward into an intense close-up on the Doctor's face. When the laser beams flare, the camera pulls back slightly with subtle kinetic vibration and lens flare bloom, tracking down toward the puddle of bubbling molten bronze metal, before panning quickly to the surviving Dalek.

## 5. Lighting & Palette
Moody industrial teal-and-slate ambience punctuated by harsh amber emergency strobe lights. Intense, blinding neon-cyan and golden glow cast over the Doctor's face and the metallic corridor walls when optic lasers fire. Volumetric steam catches the glowing heat haze and flying sparks.

## 6. Sound Clause
- `[0s-5s]`: Mechanical motor whirring and echoing metallic Dalek screech: "EXTERMINATE! THE DOCTOR HAS NO WEAPONS! SURRENDER!"
- `[5s-10s]`: Doctor sighs casually with dry British delivery: "You know, after fifteen regenerations, you'd think I'd remember the new features..."
- `[10s-18s]`: High-pitched electrical charging hum followed by a deafening, bass-heavy sci-fi laser blast `[12s]`. Crackling sizzling sounds of molten metal bubbling and sizzling steam `[14s-18s]`.
- `[18s-24s]`: Dalek death-screech glitching into silence: "DOES NOT COMPU-- *Gurgle*". The second Dalek hastily rotates its eyestalk with panicked frantic mechanical beeping: "EMERGENCY RETREAT! HE HAS EYE BEAMS!"
- `[24s-30s]`: Doctor blinks rapidly, rubbing his eyelids with his sleeve: "Ouch. A bit dry. Really need to start using eye drops." Soft dramatic synth chord swells and fades out.

## 7. Final Beat / Composition
The Doctor stands in three-quarters profile holding his smoking eyes slightly closed, while smoke rises from a molten puddle of Dalek armor in the foreground, with the surviving Dalek squealing away into darkness.

## 8. Negative Directions
Negative: No morphing artifacts, no extra limbs, no deformed hands, no floating disjointed Dalek parts, no garbled subtitles, no amateur cartoon styling, no plastic textures, no abrupt camera jumps, no low-resolution compression.
