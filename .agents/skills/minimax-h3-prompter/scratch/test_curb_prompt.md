# MiniMax H3 8-Part Playbook — Curb Your Enthusiasm: The Pink Room

### 1. Reference Job Assignments
Image 1 is Larry David: maintain balding silver hair fringe, iconic round tortoiseshell glasses, dark navy blazer over charcoal crewneck t-shirt, and expressive facial geometry. Image 2 is the guest bedroom interior: modern Brentwood estate room drenched in aggressive matte bubblegum-pink paint across walls, moldings, and electrical outlet covers.

### 2. Scene / Format / Mood
Live-action handheld television comedy, single continuous tracking master shot, 24fps, cinematic 16:9. Bright daytime natural California daylight streaming through French doors into a sunlit hallway and guest bedroom. The mood is neurotic, fast-paced, and darkly comedic.

### 3. One Dominant Action
Larry David paces along the hallway toward the doorway of the hot-pink guest bedroom with his smartphone pressed to his ear, gesticulating furiously with his free hand as he vents to Jeff on the phone.

### 4. Camera Path & Framing
The camera starts in a medium shot following Larry in a slight handheld follow-dolly as he paces down the hardwood corridor, subtly panning and pushing in toward his waist-up profile as he stops at the doorway to gesture into the vividly pink room.

### 5. Lighting & Palette
Crisp high-key Southern California sunlight bouncing off warm hardwood floors, starkly contrasted against the blinding saturated bubblegum-pink interior of the guest bedroom. Soft daylight fills Larry's face with clean specular highlights on his round glasses.

### 6. Sound Clause
[0s-4s] Natural interior room tone with subtle footsteps on wood; Larry's agitated, raspy voice: "Jeff, she painted it pink. Pepto-Bismol pink! Who does that?!" [4s-7s] Muffled tinny speakerphone audio from Jeff: "Wait, Trisha painted the whole guest room?" [7s-13s] Larry stammers and raises his voice in escalating outrage: "The walls, Jeff! The baseboards! Even the light switch plates! It's like Barbie threw up in a crime scene!" [13s-16s] Phone speaker buzzes: "Susie loves it, Larry." [16s-20s] Larry scoffs in disbelief with an exasperated shrug: "Susie loves it?! It's a hostile takeover of my guest room! It's insane!"

### 7. Final Beat / Composition
Larry freezes with his jaw dropped, holding the phone away from his ear in sheer exasperation, framed on the left third with the glaring pink bedroom occupying the background.

no morphing, no extra limbs, no AI distortion, no digital skin smoothing, no cartoon shading, no subtitles, no watermarks, no camera telephoto warping, no duplicate characters.
