# MiniMax H3 8-Part Playbook — Tokyo Station Loop: Kiwi Girl

### 1. Reference Job Assignments
Image 0 is the young New Zealand woman: maintain dirty-blonde hair tied in a messy bun, oversized khaki windbreaker jacket, black backpack straps over shoulders, tired wide blue eyes, and expressive exhausted facial geometry. Image 1 is the Tokyo subway corridor: sterile, brightly lit subterranean passageway with cream ceramic tiled walls, polished gray linoleum floor reflecting overhead fluorescent tube lights, yellow tactile paving strips, and overhead directional signage alongside a Boss Coffee vending machine.

### 2. Scene / Format / Mood
Live-action cinematic comedy, single continuous Steadicam tracking shot, 24fps, 16:9. Bright sterile cool fluorescent overhead lighting in a vast, liminal Tokyo underground station labyrinth. The mood is manic, claustrophobic, darkly comedic, and surreal.

### 3. One Dominant Action
The New Zealand woman marches briskly down the endless tiled subway corridor clutching a crinkled paper map, repeatedly glancing around in growing existential panic before slumping back against the tiled wall.

### 4. Camera Path & Framing
The camera tracks backwards smoothly in front of the woman in a medium close-up as she walks forward through the symmetrical subterranean hallway, slowly tilting down slightly as she slides her back down against the ceramic tiled wall into a defeated crouch.

### 5. Lighting & Palette
Sterile high-key cool fluorescent ceiling strips creating rhythmic reflections across the polished linoleum floor, punctuated by the glowing illuminated blue-and-white lightbox of the vending machine. Balanced skin tones with subtle under-eye fatigue shadows.

### 6. Sound Clause
[0s-4s] Distant reverberant station chime melody echoing down empty tiled tunnels; the girl mutters with a sharp New Zealand Kiwi accent: "Right, sweet as, follow the yellow line to Exit B-Seven..." [4s-10s] Squeak of rubber sneakers on polished linoleum; she pauses, eyes darting in horror: "Wait... Tommy Lee Jones vending machine, didn't I just see you two minutes ago?!" [10s-15s] Fluorescent tube hum and soft echo; she clutches her head: "I've passed this exact ramen poster four times! The noodle is mocking me!" [15s-20s] Resigned sigh as fabric rustles against tile: "Yeah, nah... I live here now. I am the station ghost."

### 7. Final Beat / Composition
The camera settles into a low-angle medium shot of the girl sitting slumped against the tiled wall clutching her knees, dwarfed by the towering perspective lines of the infinite, empty subway corridor stretching behind her.

### 8. Negative Directions
no morphing, no extra limbs, no AI distortion, no digital cartoon look, no blurred faces, no floating props, no subtitles, no watermarks, no camera stutter, no duplicate characters.
