# MiniMax H3 Production Brief: Pink UFO Slimes House

## 1. Reference Job Assignments
- Image 1 (<Picture 1>): Reference photograph of the house, strictly defining the building's architecture, exterior materials, window placement, roof structure, front yard, and ambient daylight illumination.

## 2. Scene / Format / Mood
Cinematic live-action photorealistic sci-fi scene with natural daylight. Setting is a peaceful suburban residential home established from Image 1. The tone shifts dynamically from quiet suburban serenity to rapid sci-fi intrusion, settling into a surreal, dripping aftermath.

## 3. One Dominant Action
A metallic pink flying saucer buzzes low over the roofline of the house, releasing a torrential spray of thick neon-pink slime across the facade, before banking smoothly upward into the sky and leaving the building covered in dripping pink goo.

## 4. Camera Path & Framing
The shot begins in a wide, stable cinematic establishing frame of the house matching Image 1. As the UFO swoops in from the upper-left, the camera executes a slight upward tilt following the aircraft's fast trajectory, then tilts back down with a continuous slow push in toward the house, settling into a stable frame that highlights the viscous fluid dynamics as the slime cascades down the roof and walls.

## 5. Lighting & Palette
Natural late-afternoon sun (golden hour 5000K) casting soft realistic shadows across the house facade and lawn. The natural suburban palette of earthy brick, dark roof tiles, and green grass is dramatically contrasted by the hyper-vivid, bioluminescent hot-pink slime and glowing magenta thruster lights of the UFO.

## 6. Sound Clause
- [0s-2s]: Quiet outdoor ambience with gentle breeze rustling tree leaves and distant suburban birdsong.
- [2s-5s]: Low vibrating anti-gravity hum escalating into a deafening high-velocity jet whoosh [3s] as the UFO buzzes close over the roofline.
- [5s-8s]: Heavy wet splattering, visceral gelatinous squelches, and rushing fluid torrents as the thick pink slime smacks the roof shingles and windows.
- [8s-12s]: Fading sci-fi engine hum echoing into the upper atmosphere, transitioning into continuous wet dripping, bubbling, and gooey plops onto the concrete and grass below.

## 7. Final Beat / Composition
The shot concludes on a static wide shot of the house, fully coated in thick ribbons of dripping, high-gloss pink slime that pool on the porch roof and slowly drip from the eaves onto the front lawn.

## 8. Negative Directions
No morphing of the house structure, no alterations to the original architecture from Image 1, no cartoon or cel-shaded animation styling, no low-resolution textures, no synthetic digital artifacts, no camera jump cuts or stuttering, no floating ungrounded slime particles.
