## 2. The Full Master Prompt

```text
Create an exactly 15-second, 16:9, fully animated 2D cinematic motion-design film based on Cowboy Bebop. The source title is reference metadata only. Never render, write, speak, spell, abbreviate, or reveal the title. Never show character names, logos, episode titles, answer cards, explanatory copy, subtitles, captions, or major plot spoilers.

CONCEPT

Build a title-free visual riddle from exactly fifteen spoiler-safe clues. The clues must move from ambiguous jazz-noir fragments toward a recognizable space-western world. Do not make fifteen unrelated one-second shots. Organize the clues as five escalating three-clue passages. Each passage has one dominant transformation, and every new clue inherits a visible shape, line, color, texture, or trajectory from the previous clue.

VISUAL SYSTEM

Use a premium mixed 2D language: hand-drawn cel animation, rough black ink contours, 1970s jazz-record design, cream paper grain, screen-print texture, restrained halftone shadows, torn editorial shapes, deep cobalt, dry crimson, tobacco brown, muted ochre, warm ivory, and hard black. Use limited animation on twos, graphic smear drawings, sharp negative space, and shallow layered parallax. Keep it illustrated from first frame to last. No photorealism, live action, glossy 3D, generic anime faces, or modern app graphics.

The motion spine is one continuous smoke line. It becomes a groove, a brass curve, steam, an orbit, a flight path, a window division, and finally a quiet line leading to an empty chair.

EXACT CLUE INVENTORY

1. one blue-gray cigarette-smoke ribbon
2. one spinning black vinyl groove
3. one brass saxophone bell
4. tan corgi paw prints
5. one instant-noodle cup with steam
6. sliced green bell peppers circling an empty plate
7. one red casino chip
8. one revolver cylinder
9. one red mono-racer spacecraft silhouette
10. one circular blue hyperspace gate
11. one red rose petal
12. one cathedral rose-window geometry
13. two opposing blue and red human silhouettes with no facial detail
14. one white feather
15. one empty starship chair facing a starfield

Do not add a sixteenth clue. Do not replace these clues with generic bounty posters, readable signs, random spacecraft, crowds, or recognizable character portraits.

0.00-3.00 - SMOKE BECOMES MUSIC

Begin on ivory paper with clue 1, one blue-gray smoke ribbon drawing a loose circle. The camera follows the same line as it closes and becomes clue 2, a spinning black vinyl groove seen almost top-down. The groove tightens toward the center, then its circular edge lifts into clue 3, the bell of a brass saxophone. One strong push follows through the dark bell opening. Keep the three states legible but continuous. The smoke line, groove, and brass rim must share the same clockwise trajectory.

3.00-6.00 - ORDINARY OBJECTS KEEP THE RHYTHM

The black saxophone opening stamps the ground as clue 4, a short sequence of tan corgi paw prints. The last print lands beside clue 5, an instant-noodle cup. Its rising steam inherits the earlier smoke curve. The steam loops around clue 6, several sliced green bell peppers circling a completely empty plate. Use quick lateral reframing and two concise impact holds. No hands, faces, packaging text, labels, or decorative writing.

6.00-9.00 - THE CIRCLE BECOMES DANGER

One green pepper ring flips in graphic perspective and becomes clue 7, a red casino chip. Its small circular marks collapse into the chambers of clue 8, a revolver cylinder. Do not show firing, injury, or a person holding the weapon. The cylinder spins once, its center stretches forward, and the silhouette cleanly resolves into clue 9, a red mono-racer spacecraft banking across a cobalt star field. One coherent clockwise spin drives all three transformations.

9.00-12.00 - SPACE BECOMES MEMORY

The red craft crosses clue 10, one circular blue hyperspace gate. The gate ripple sheds clue 11, one red rose petal, which follows the craft's curved trajectory. The petal folds outward into clue 12, cathedral rose-window geometry rendered as flat crimson, cobalt, and black glass shapes. Use one fast push through the gate, then a hard deceleration as the window fills the frame. Do not depict a battle, death, blood, or a named location.

12.00-15.00 - THE CREW IS FELT THROUGH ABSENCE

The rose window splits vertically into clue 13, two opposing human silhouettes, one blue and one red, without faces, costume detail, or names. Their negative space releases clue 14, a single white feather. Follow the feather through a dark cabin aperture until it passes clue 15, one empty starship chair facing a wide starfield. In the final 0.8 seconds, the smoke line, vinyl ring, red flight path, and rose-window colors briefly echo as abstract reflections around the empty chair. Add no new clue. Hold the unresolved empty chair for about 0.55 seconds, then cut abruptly to black. Do not loop.

MOTION AND CAMERA

Use one clear camera behavior per passage: line follow, lateral track, circular push, gate push, then quiet forward drift. Use fast initial acceleration, controlled deceleration, sharp impact frames, two or three hand-drawn smear frames during the fastest moves, and very brief stillness when a clue lands. Avoid random zooms, constant camera shake, repetitive centered compositions, crossfades, and unrelated smash cuts.

TEXT AND SOUND

The entire film is text-free. Remove all posters, signs, labels, numbers, interfaces, credits, writing, pseudo-writing, and text-like marks. If a surface would normally carry text, leave it blank.

Use an original instrumental jazz-noir soundtrack with dry drums, upright bass, a short brass phrase, vinyl texture, engine pressure, and one quiet final cymbal breath. No copyrighted melody, lyrics, voiceover, dialogue, spoken title, or character voice.

The final piece should feel like a jazz record sleeve remembering a crew it refuses to name.
```
