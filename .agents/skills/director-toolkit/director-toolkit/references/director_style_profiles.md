# Director Style Profiles & Blending Guide

A technical reference detailing the camera, lighting, blocking, and acoustic configurations of archetypal cinematic directors. Designed for modular application and cross-pollination.

---

## 1. Wes Anderson — The Symmetrical Control-Freak

### The Psychological Engine
Aesthetic perfectionism and rigid planar symmetry acting as emotional armor to contain grief, neurosis, or childhood loss. The world is tightly arranged so nothing unpredictable can hurt the characters.

### Technical Profile
- **Lens & Camera:** 28mm–35mm prime lenses. Strict planar one-point perspective; subjects centered on the vertical axis. Locked-off tripod or heavy dolly tracks on a 90° or 180° grid. Strict 90° whip-pans.
- **Lighting & Color:** High-key, shadowless, flat illumination. Curated monochromatic pastel palettes (mustard yellow, dusty rose, mint green, powder blue).
- **Blocking & Acting:** Tableau vivant. Actors face the camera squarely at 90° like vintage portraits. Deadpan, monotone vocal delivery; zero theatrical melodrama.
- **Sound & Music:** Dry, intimate, close-miked dialogue without room reverb. Vintage orchestral or 60s European pop needle-drops.

## 2. Terry Gilliam — The Bureaucratic Grotesque

### The Psychological Engine
The fragile, romantic individual smothered and deformed by a monolithic, malfunctioning, Kafkaesque institutional machine.

### Technical Profile
- **Lens & Camera:** 14mm–21mm ultra-wide lenses positioned unusually close to human faces, exaggerating facial features and warping spatial depth. Low-angle Dutch tilts, canted angles, and a restless, roaming camera.
- **Lighting & Color:** Murky institutional 4300K fluorescent greens, cold industrial cyans, and sickly sodium ambers. Deep, dirty shadows with little fill light.
- **Blocking & Mis-en-scène:** Extreme visual clutter. Pneumatic tubes, frayed wires, CRT monitors, and paper stacks physically obstructing the frame. Humans dwarfed by towering bureaucratic architecture.
- **Sound & Atmosphere:** Auditory claustrophobia. Clattering manual typewriters, whirring ventilation ducts, steam hisses, and distorted echoing public address announcements.

## 3. James Cameron — Industrial Kinetic Dread

### The Psychological Engine
Fragile human biology, blue-collar workers, and military discipline trapped inside claustrophobic, high-pressure industrial slaughterhouses.

### Technical Profile
- **Lens & Camera:** 50mm–85mm focal lengths. Tight kinetic framing that boxes characters into functional spaces. Relentless forward camera movement through corridors; handheld jitter during physical breaches.
- **Lighting & Color:** Low-key chiaroscuro with extreme contrast. Industrial sodium vapor (589nm amber) clashing with steely blue-cyan emergency backlights. Rotating red emergency beacons, practical work lights, wet metallic surfaces reflecting hard light.
- **Blocking & Action:** Functional, tactical blocking. Characters pinned against bulkheads or trapped in narrow maintenance crawlways. Heavy hydraulic machinery or cargo doors framing the scene.
- **Sound & Music:** Heavy mechanical pulse. Rhythmic hydraulic thuds, echoing sonar pings, muffled klaxons, heavy breathing through respirators, and relentless percussive synth basslines.

## 4. 80s Broadcast / BBC Prestige Drama — Somatic Restraint

### The Psychological Engine
Kitchen-sink realism and emotional stoicism. Drama generated by what is withheld rather than what is spoken.

### Technical Profile
- **Lens & Camera:** Stationary broadcast studio pedestal. Motorized parfocal optical zoom (35mm to 105mm) that creeps imperceptibly into a close-up, compressing depth without 3D parallax.
- **Lighting & Color:** Practical 3200K tungsten halogen lamps. Amber lampshade glow washing over dark wood paneling, linoleum, and laminate tables. Velvety tobacco shadows without digital shadow lift.
- **Blocking & Pacing:** Unhurried two-shots. Characters staring at everyday objects (teacups, cigarette ashtrays). 1.5s–3.0s dead air pauses between lines.
- **Medium Texture:** 1-inch Type C tape, 576i PAL broadcast raster, soft optical rolloff, and Plumbicon tube glow.
- **Sound & Atmosphere:** Raw domestic room tone. Refrigerator hum, mantel clock tick, distant rainfall, zero cinematic musical score.

---

## 5. Simon Stålenhag — Retro-Futuristic Techno-Melancholy / Monolithic Ruin

### The Psychological Engine
Pastoral melancholia and mundane rural normalcy coexisting with decaying, colossal technological infrastructure. The monumental era of cybernetic hubris has already peaked, leaving behind massive rusting monoliths that humans treat with indifferent familiarity.

### Technical Profile
- **Lens & Camera:** 28mm–45mm wide-to-normal focal lengths. Deep focus capturing both foreground human figures and monumental deep-background structures without extreme telephoto compression. Low-angle compositions looking up at towering tech monuments anchoring the horizon.
- **Lighting & Color:** Diffused, overcast winter daylight; low pale desaturated sun; muted earth tones (muddy ochre, dead straw grass, cold damp asphalt) contrasted with cold steel cyans and sudden glowing accents (warm amber beacon strobes, crimson status LEDs, pulsating neural cables).
- **Blocking & Mis-en-scène:** Environmental Anchor Replacement—substituting generic landscape markers (bare trees, rolling hills) with narrative techno-relics (towering neuro-relay spires, half-buried excavator walkers). Human figures dwarfed by rusted industrial mass, walking down rural highways alongside colossal derelict machines.
- **Sound & Atmosphere:** Sparse, somber acoustic bed. Distant 50Hz electrical transformer hum, wind howling through exposed tubular steel framing, wet gravel crunching under tires/boots, and low-frequency resonant mechanical groans echoing across flat fields.

## 6. Style Blending Playbook (Cross-Pollination)

You are never locked into a single director. The power of this toolkit is combining the grammar of one director with the atmosphere of another:

| Blend Name | Formulation | Resulting Dramatic Aesthetic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Pastel Dread"** | Anderson Composition + Cameron Lighting | A perfectly centered, pastel-tiled laboratory suddenly invaded by a slow creeping shadow and rotating industrial red strobes. |
| **"Bureaucratic Kitchen-Sink"** | Gilliam Optics + 80s Restraint | An unblinking clerk stared down by an ultra-wide 18mm lens under buzzing fluorescent tubes, holding dead air for four seconds. |
| **"Industrial Symmetry"** | Cameron Environments + Anderson Staging | Two heavily armored space-freighter technicians standing at dead-center 90° angles inside a gigantic, rust-streaked mechanical hangar. |
| **"Pastoral Relic Melancholy"** | Stålenhag Scale + 80s Broadcast Restraint | A lone rural pickup truck idling on a gravel road under a massive humming power tower, holding 4 seconds of cold wind and engine tick. |
| **"Monolithic Kinetic War"** | Stålenhag Environmental Relics + Cameron Action | A brutal, multi-combatant mechanical clash staged across an open winter field, strictly anchored around a colossal rusted transmission tower with zero spatial cheating. |
