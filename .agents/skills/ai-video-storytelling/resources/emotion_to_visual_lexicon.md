# Emotion-to-Visual Translation Lexicon

## 1. Directing Philosophy: Deconstructing Emotion into Physical Signatures

Generative AI video diffusion models (e.g., MiniMax H3, Hailuo, Runway Gen-3) are optical pattern predictors trained on visual media. They do not possess psychological comprehension. When presented with emotional adjectives (*"she is heartbroken," "he looks guilty," "the atmosphere is tense"*), the models default to theatrical melodrama, morphing faces, or inconsistent expressions across seeds.

To achieve precise, repeatable, and nuanced emotional performances, you must decompose every emotional state into **5 Measurable Optical/Physical Channels**:

1. **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Gaze coordinates, saccades, blink frequency, ocular moisture, swallowing reflex, nasal flare, jaw clenching, lip compression, respiration depth/rate, finger pressure, trembling.
2. **Postural Dynamics:** Spinal curvature (C-curve flexion vs. rigid thoracic extension), shoulder girdle elevation, head tilt/yaw, weight distribution (balls of feet vs. back heels), center of gravity.
3. **Proxemics & Spatial Occlusion:** Interpersonal distance (intimate <0.5m, personal 0.5–1.2m, social >1.5m), physical barriers (crossed arms, clutching bags/cups, furniture shielding), torso angulation (facing direct vs. 45° bladed away).
4. **Lighting & Specular Atmosphere:** Key-to-fill lighting ratio (e.g., high-contrast 8:1 vs. flat 2:1), color temperature (Kelvin), shadow placement (deep eye socket hollows vs. open illumination), specular highlights (sweat sheen, wet tear tracks, dull matte dryness).
5. **Camera Behavior & Optical Vector:** Lens focal length (e.g., 24mm wide angle for spatial distortion/claustrophobia vs. 85mm prime for emotional isolation/compression), camera height (low-angle dominance vs. high-angle vulnerability), movement speed, trajectory (slow creeping push-in vs. static surveillance lock vs. handheld jitter).

## 2. The 5-Channel Emotional Translation Dictionary

### 1. Guarded / Suppressed Hostility
* **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Gaze fixed 15° off-axis from listener; zero blinking for 4+ seconds; jaw masseter muscle visibly flexing under skin; lips pressed into a bloodless horizontal line; shallow, high-chest respiration where only collarbones rise.
* **Postural Dynamics:** Rigid spine held completely off chair backrest; shoulders pinned back and elevated; chin tucked slightly downward; hands clamped into tight fists in lap or pockets.
* **Proxemics & Occlusion:** Torso angled 45° away from counterpart; forearms crossed tightly over ribcage; a heavy drinking glass held firmly as a chest-height barrier.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Harsh 4:1 key-to-fill ratio; cold 5600K overhead fluorescent light casting sharp horizontal nose shadows; deep eye-socket shadows obscuring pupils; subtle sweat sheen on upper lip.
* **Camera & Optics:** 85mm telephoto compression; eye-level, perfectly static framing; creeping slow push-in (1 inch per second) locking the subject in center frame without cuts.
* **Audio / Foley:** Total silence punctuated by tight, audible nasal exhalations, the faint grinding click of molars, and a heavy glass setting down hard on wood.

---

### 2. Grief / Crushing Sadness (Internalized vs. Visceral)
* **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Heavy eyelids drooping at 45°; gaze unfocused and fixed on the floor 3 feet ahead; slow, deliberate single blink lasting 0.5 seconds; heavy swallowing reflex with visible larynx drop; trembling lower lip held stationary; wet ocular sheen gathering at lower eyelid margins without streaming.
* **Postural Dynamics:** Pronounced thoracic C-curve spine collapse; slumped shoulders rounded forward; head hanging low; weight heavily grounded on flat heels; arms hanging loosely with palms facing backwards.
* **Proxemics & Occlusion:** Self-isolation in large negative space; curled into room corners; knees pulled up toward chest; hands clutching opposite elbows.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Muted, low-contrast 2:1 fill; overcast 6500K daylight through rain-streaked window; soft desaturated blue-grey grading; diffuse, wet reflections on cheekbones.
* **Camera & Optics:** 50mm vintage prime; high-angle downward perspective; slow drifting pull-out accentuating isolation in empty architectural space.
* **Audio / Foley:** Long, shuddering exhalation through parted lips; damp sniffle; quiet room tone and rain patter against glass.

---

### 3. Performed Calm (The Social Mask / Practiced Concealment)
* **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Symmetrical, static social smile with lips closed; eyes wide and completely unblinking; fixed forward gaze making direct eye contact with no saccades; rapid, invisible micro-breaths through nose.
* **Postural Dynamics:** Perfectly upright posture; hands clasped neatly on tabletop; shoulders relaxed and lowered artificially; zero fidgeting or involuntary micro-twitches.
* **Proxemics & Occlusion:** Open body language directed straight at the counterparty; arms resting on open table surfaces; eliminating physical barriers to project artificial safety.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Flat, warm 3200K high-key commercial lighting; even illumination removing all harsh shadows; bright white catchlights in both pupils.
* **Camera & Optics:** 35mm medium shot at chest level; steady, balanced symmetrical framing; static camera holding steady without movement.
* **Audio / Foley:** Smooth, modulated vocal delivery; quiet clinking of porcelain teacups; crisp, artificial pleasantries without voice cracking.

---

### 4. Paranoia / Hypervigilance
* **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Rapid, darting eye saccades scanning periphery every 1.5 seconds; wide palpebral fissures (whites visible above irises); flared nostrils; rapid shallow breathing (30+ breaths/min); trembling fingers repeatedly checking watch, door handle, or collar.
* **Postural Dynamics:** Forward weight bias on the balls of the feet; neck craning forward; asymmetrical shoulder posture ready to pivot or sprint.
* **Proxemics & Occlusion:** Back pressed firmly against a solid wall; constantly orienting face toward room entrances/doors; pacing in tight 1-meter radius.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** High-contrast chiaroscuro 8:1 ratio; stark amber streetlamps through venetian blinds slicing horizontal shadow bars across the face; deep pitch-black shadows.
* **Camera & Optics:** 24mm wide-angle lens close to the face causing subtle perspective barrel distortion; handheld organic micro-jitter (unstable operator breathing); Dutch tilt (5–8° roll).
* **Audio / Foley:** Elevated heartbeat thumping in sub-bass; sharp fabric rustle on every sudden head turn; high-frequency fluorescent tube buzzing.

---

### 5. Suppressed Rage (Volcanic Pre-Detonation)
* **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Direct predator eye-lock; pupils dilated; flared nostrils; jaw clenched so tightly the temporal arteries pulse visibly; lips pulled back slightly exposing clenched teeth; white-knuckled grip with tendons straining in forearms.
* **Postural Dynamics:** Forward-leaning torso; chest expanded; shoulders square and locked; chin jutting forward; weight planted wide in aggressive stance.
* **Proxemics & Occlusion:** Encroaching aggressively across personal space boundary (<0.4m); towering over seated counterpart; removing all physical barriers.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Hard directional side key (3000K warm tungsten) casting dramatic split lighting; harsh specular sweat beads across temple; deep red-tinted ambient background.
* **Camera & Optics:** 35mm low-angle heroic shot; rapid, aggressive push-in halting abruptly 18 inches from the subject's face.
* **Audio / Foley:** Heavy, ragged nasal breathing like forced bellows; low guttural vocal timbre; the creak of strained leather or tight fabric.

---

### 6. Guilt & Shame (The Burden of Exposure)
* **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Gaze instantly averts downward and to the right whenever addressed; frequent rapid blinking (4 blinks in 2 seconds); hand repeatedly touching back of neck, face, or collarbone; nervous dry swallow; downward corner of mouth twitch.
* **Postural Dynamics:** Shrinking posture; shoulders hunched inward to protect throat and chest; head tilted downward; shifting weight uncomfortably from foot to foot.
* **Proxemics & Occlusion:** Backing away toward edges of frame; turning side profile to speaker; crossing one arm over abdomen while other hand shields mouth.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Top-down downlighting casting heavy brow shadows over eyes; cold desaturated palette; isolated spotlighting in dark surrounding space.
* **Camera & Optics:** 85mm long lens framing subject through doorframes or between foreground objects (voyeuristic dirty framing); slow lateral track across foreground obstacles.
* **Audio / Foley:** Hesitant vocal cadence with audible throat clearing; soft shuffling footwear; quiet ambient room tone.

---

### 7. Sudden Shock / Acute Cognitive Trauma
* **Somatic & Micro-Kinetics:** Complete facial immobilization; jaw drops open 1 centimeter; eyes frozen wide in a glassy 1000-yard stare; total cessation of blinking; breathing freezes mid-inhalation with chest locked high; color draining from face.
* **Postural Dynamics:** Total muscular catatonia; arms frozen in whatever position they were held; knees slightly unlocked; weight balance frozen.
* **Proxemics & Occlusion:** Complete unawareness of surrounding space, proximity, or hazards; dropping held objects (pen, keys, glass) without noticing.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Harsh stark white 6000K flash or overexposed bloom; washed-out high key transitioning into cold shadow.
* **Camera & Optics:** Rapid Dolly-Zoom (Vertigo effect: push in on lens while zooming out, keeping head size identical as background warps); extreme close-up on dilated pupils.
* **Audio / Foley:** Sudden high-pitched tinnitus ring (4kHz sine wave) suppressing all ambient foley into muffled underwater murk; single isolated heavy heartbeat sound.
