# AI Video Storytelling & Acting Engine

## Overview
This skill bridges the "Director-to-AI Void" by translating abstract emotional intent ("sad," "tense," "guarded") into concrete, photographable physical and optical signatures. It provides a complete directing pipeline for generative AI video, specializing in character continuity, narrative state machines, non-linear/dream cutting, and direct prompt compilation.

## The 6 Pillars
1. **Emotion-to-Visual Translation:** Converts emotional adjectives into 5 concrete channels (Somatic/Micro-Kinetics, Posture, Proxemics/Occlusion, Lighting/Atmosphere, Camera Vectors).
2. **Character Continuity System:** Enforces a 6-field verbatim Character Bible schema across shots to prevent facial, wardrobe, and lighting drift.
3. **State-Trigger Emotional Architecture:** Models scenes as Finite State Machines ($S_1, S_2$) with defined triggers and transition velocities (*Instant Snap <0.5s* vs *Lagged Bleed 1.5–3.0s*).
4. **Non-Linear & Dream-Style Cutting:** Employs tactile motif anchors, the 80/20 Loop-and-Variation engine, match-cut matrices, and musical beat-mapped pacing.
5. **Model-Specific Prompt Compiler:** Compiles storyboard tables directly into MiniMax H3 (both 6-Section Reference Model and 8-Part Playbook).
6. **Storyboard-as-Instrument:** Production-ready markdown workbooks acting as the single source of truth for planning and generating.
7. **Negative Space, Conversational Latency & Metaphorical Displacement:** Directs 1.5s–3.0s cognitive processing delays between spoken lines, acoustic room tone hygiene (banning wall-to-wall muzak), and mundane displacement activities where physical objects act as emotional heat sinks.

## How to Use
Ask the agent to direct, storyboard, or translate an emotional sequence into generative video prompts:

**Example Prompts:**
- "Use the ai-video-storytelling skill to storyboard an argument between two detectives that snaps into performed calm when a suspect walks in."
- "Translate the emotional arc of grief into a 5-channel physical signature for a 15-second single shot."
- "Create a Character Bible and 3-shot state trigger sequence for a neo-noir interrogation."
