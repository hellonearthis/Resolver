# Psychological Design — Cognitive Writing & Audience Pacing Engine

A meta-craft framework and engineering system that treats writing not merely as text on a page, but as **an instruction set for the reader's cognitive architecture**.

Psychological design operates on the fundamental principle that every piece of writing manipulates three core variables:
1. **Expectation:** What the reader's mind predicts will happen next.
2. **Attention:** What is explicitly highlighted vs. what is held in subtext or background friction.
3. **Identification:** How closely the reader projects themselves into a character's state vs. maintaining ironic distance.

## 🛠️ The "Tools, Not Rules" Cognitive Architecture

A foundational rule of psychological design is that **dramatic and comedic rules are cognitive expectation tools, not binding recipes**:
* **The Cognitive Function:** Beat sheets, 3-act arcs, and the Rule of Three exist because human cognition is an active prediction machine. Structure creates anticipation; satisfying or subverting that anticipation creates dopamine and emotional resonance.
* **The Dual Traps:**
  1. *Dogmatic Formula Adherence:* Slavishly following formulas turns writing into a predictable recipe that bores the audience.
  2. *Formless Anarchy:* Discarding all structure eliminates the cognitive baseline, leaving the audience disoriented and uninvested.
* **Structural Bending:** Master writers use rules as malleable tools—syncopating beats, delaying pivots, using micro-domino escalations, or colliding contrasting styles (e.g. Terry Gilliam Dutch chaos colliding with Wes Anderson planimetric rigidity).

## 🛠️ Application Modes

### Mode 1: Comedic Cognitive Engineering
* Cross-references `comedy-craft`, `comedy-tv-writer`, and `inclusive-comedy-guardrails`.
* Ensures setups build Frame A without trailing filler.
* Places the subversion on the final word ("kick word").
* Leverages benign violation so the joke punches up or targets self-deprecation without causing genuine audience threat.

### Mode 2: Poetic Lineation & Volta Audit
* Cross-references `poetry-craft`.
* Audits line breaks to ensure enjambment creates active cognitive suspense.
* Evaluates the volta/turn to ensure the emotional shift is earned through concrete sensory imagery.

### Mode 3: Narrative Scene & Subtext Analysis
* Cross-references `comedy-tv-writer`, `ai-video-storytelling`, and `book-to-video-prompt`.
* Maps the scene's starting vs. ending value state.
* Removes on-the-nose explanations where dialogue states what the subtext already conveys.
* Calibrates scale mismatch (understatement vs. escalation).

### Mode 4: Script Doctor Diagnostic & Feedback Protocol
* Cross-references `references/screenwriting_pitfalls_and_diagnostics.md`, `character-persona-architect`, and `grill-me`.
* **Symptom vs. Root Cause Translation:** Isolates reader complaints (e.g., *"dialogue is stiff"*, *"the scene drags"*) to diagnose the underlying mechanical flaw (missing subtext/shame, absence of value shift, or robotic formula adherence).
* **Individuality Shield:** Restructures scene engines while fiercely protecting idiosyncratic character quirks, tactile specifics, and unique authorial voice from being sanded down into generic competence.
* **Non-Defensive Diagnostic Frame:** Re-frames all feedback as telemetry reporting where cognitive immersion lapsed, empowering targeted mechanical refinement.
